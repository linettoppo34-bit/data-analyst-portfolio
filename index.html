Retail Sales and Profit Dashboard

Python (pandas, matplotlib) · Sample Superstore dataset

Which categories, regions, and discount levels make money, and which lose it?

How I approach it
  • Check for missing values and remove 17 duplicate rows.
  • Calculate sales, profit, and margin, then break them down by category, region, state, and discount level.
  • Chart the results in a one-page dashboard.
  • Write three recommendations based on what the numbers show.
Finding: order lines discounted by more than 20% lost $135K in total, while lines at 20% or less earned $422K. Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies lose money overall. Dashboard PDF · Code and README

E-commerce Delivery and Customer Reviews

SQL (SQLite) and Python · Olist Brazilian E-commerce dataset

Do late deliveries lower review scores, and by how much?

How I approach it
  • Join the orders, reviews, and customers tables with SQL.
  • Compare promised and actual delivery dates to flag late orders.
  • Group review scores by delay length and by product category.
  • Summarize the findings in a one-page report.
Finding: late deliveries cut the average review score from 4.29 to 2.57 out of 5. 54% of late orders got a 1 or 2 star review, against 9% of on-time orders. Report PDF · SQL file · README

Employee Attrition Analysis

Python (pandas, matplotlib) · IBM HR Analytics sample dataset

Which groups of employees are most likely to leave, and what do they have in common?

How I approach it
  • Calculate the attrition rate by job role, overtime, age, tenure, and income band.
  • Compare pay, overtime, and job satisfaction between those who left and those who stayed.
  • Present the biggest risk groups in a one-page dashboard.
